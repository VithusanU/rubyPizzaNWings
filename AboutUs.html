About Us

A neighbourhood kitchen in the heart of the Upper Beaches

Where Pizza Meets the World

Ruby Pizza N' Wings is a family-run restaurant at 162 Main Street in Toronto's east end. We started with a simple idea: take the pizza and wings everyone loves, and bring in the flavours we grew up with.

That's why our menu goes beyond the classics. Alongside pepperoni pizza and honey garlic wings, you'll find butter chicken pizza, curry chicken pizza, shawarma plates, souvlaki, poutine, and fresh pasta — Asian and international cuisine, made from scratch daily.

Whether it's a date night, a family game night, or just a quick slice, we've got a combo for it. Come say hi, or get it delivered hot to your door.
